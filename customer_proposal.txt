CUSTOMER PROJECT PROPOSAL
 Smart Table-Side Self-Ordering Kiosk
 A connected ordering, payment, kitchen and analytics experience for dine-in restaurants
Prepared for: Customer / Restaurant Management
Prepared by: Project Development Team
Proposal version: 1.0
1. Executive Summary
We propose to develop a production-ready table-side self-ordering platform that allows restaurant guests to
browse a visual menu, customize dishes, place orders, track preparation status and complete payment directly
from a kiosk/table device. The platform will also provide restaurant staff and management with operational visibility
through kitchen and management interfaces.
The solution is designed as a phased rollout: pilot deployment, restaurant-wide deployment, operational
optimization and data-driven improvement.
2. Business Objectives
 Reduce guest waiting time for order taking and payment.
 Reduce errors caused by handwritten or verbal orders.
 Give guests a visual menu with photos, prices, preparation time and customization options.
 Route orders directly to the kitchen workflow.
 Provide real-time visibility into sales, menu performance and guest feedback.
 Improve table turnover and support higher-value orders through relevant recommendations.
 Create a scalable foundation that can support multiple tables, outlets and future integrations.
3. Proposed Solution Scope
Guest / Kiosk Experience
 Visual menu with high-resolution dish images.
 Dish details including price, preparation time and spice level.
 Dish customization and special preferences.
 Cart and order review before confirmation.
 Table-aware ordering.
 Order status visibility during preparation.
 Payment at the table.
 Split-bill support.
 Optional tipping.

4. End-to-End Business Flow
1. Guest Orders — Guest browses dishes, views photos, preparation time and customization options, then places
the order.
2. Kitchen Receives — The order is routed to the kitchen workflow for preparation.
3. Meal Prepared — Kitchen staff update the order status as preparation progresses.
4. Guest Pays — The guest completes payment and, where applicable, selects tip or split-bill options.
5. Data Syncs — Order, payment and feedback information becomes available to authorized management
interfaces.
5. Delivery Approach
Phase 1: Pilot
Deploy the solution to a limited number of tables and validate the guest, kitchen and payment workflows.
Phase 2: Full Rollout
Expand deployment across the restaurant and train operational staff.
Phase 3: Optimize
Improve menu content, preparation-time accuracy, operational workflows and payment experience based on pilot
data.
Phase 4: Scale Insights
Use dashboard data to support menu, staffing and operational decisions.
6. Quality, Security & Reliability
 Role-based access for guest, kitchen/staff and management functions.
 Secure authentication and authorization for management functions.
 Protected payment processing through an appropriate payment provider.
 Auditability of important operational actions.
 Error handling and recovery for temporary network interruptions.
 Monitoring and logging for production operations.
 Backup and recovery strategy for business-critical data.
 Production testing covering kiosk, kitchen and management workflows.
